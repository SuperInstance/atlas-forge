# v0 Pre-Registration — atlas-forge

Date: 2026-10-01. Sealed BEFORE the first training run (R2).

## Engine contract (hard constraints)

- Glyph count N=70 (engine atlas slot is 73; indices 0..69 used here,
  engine reserves the last 3).
- Signature: 4 cols × 6 rows, bit = row*4 + col (row-major, row=subY).
- Serialization: threshold >0.5 → u32. No floats reach the election loop (R6).
- Drop-in: `exports/atlas_optimized.js` must be a Uint32Array literal in
  FONT_ATLAS_DATA order-compatible form.

## Loss (corrected from the proposal sketch)

L = α·L_density + β·L_sobel + γ·L_ortho + δ·L_conf

- L_density (α=1.0): sorted per-glyph ink ratios vs linspace(0.02, 0.98, N).
  Even coverage ramp; prevents duplicate-density glyphs.
- L_sobel (β=0.5): each glyph is ASSIGNED a target orientation bin
  (glyph i → bin i mod 16; 70 glyphs → ~4.4 per bin). Glyph orientation =
  magnitude-weighted circular mean of its soft-raster Sobel field.
  Loss = mean circular distance to assigned bin center. Feeds the 16-bin
  gated election (chiaroscuro Lane B). NOTE: atan2 gradient is undefined at
  the origin; Sobel field of a near-flat glyph has ~zero magnitude →
  weight by magnitude, add ε, and accept that flat glyphs' orientation is
  free (their bin loss is masked out where g < 0.05).
- L_ortho (γ=0.5): mean off-diagonal COSINE similarity of flattened
  profiles (NOT raw dot product — raw dot scales with ink and lets █
  dominate every gradient; this is the correction to the sketch).
- L_conf (δ=0.05): mean 4·p·(1−p) — pushes bits away from 0.5 so
  thresholding is stable.

## Training config (pinned)

seed=20261001, N=70, 4×6, Adam lr=0.01, epochs=3000, params init N(0,1).
CPU torch (no GPU on build host). Soft raster = sigmoid(profiles).

## Metrics (all computed on the THRESHOLDED atlas)

M1 min pairwise Hamming distance (higher better; 24 max).
M2 pairwise Hamming distinctness = unique signatures / N.
M3 ink-ramp R²: sorted ink ratios vs uniform ramp.
M4 orientation-bin occupancy: bins with ≥1 glyph above magnitude floor,
   and L1 distance of bin histogram from uniform.
M5 scene quantization error: on the chiaroscuro pinned benchmark
   (seed=20261001, 30 frames: 10 diagonal / 10 disk / 10 noise, 480×360,
   120×60 cells) — mean per-cell min-Hamming between the cell's true 4×6
   luma pattern and its elected glyph. Lower is better.
   A/B: LiberationMono-derived real atlas (font_atlas_packager 73045ae,
   re-serialized to the same 70-glyph order) vs optimized atlas.
   Null control: seed=1234 random 70×24-bit codes, same metric.

## Verdict rule (pre-registered, no moving goalposts)

SHIP (optimize + integrate) iff BOTH:
  V1: M5(optimized) < M5(LiberationMono) on ≥2 of 3 scene classes, AND
  V2: M2(optimized) ≥ M2(LiberationMono)  (no uniqueness regression).
Otherwise: DO NOT SHIP; report which clause failed and why.

## Known risks (recorded before the run)

- RISK-1: 70 codewords × 24 bits cannot all be far apart; expect min
  Hamming ~6–10, not 24. The verdict does not require beating the real
  atlas on M1.
- RISK-2: synthetic scenes may favor the optimizer's training distribution
  if losses implicitly match scene statistics. Mitigation: benchmark scenes
  are generated by an INDEPENDENT script (chiaroscuro protocol), not by
  this repo's training code.
- RISK-3: sigmoid saturation could freeze early. lr=0.01 with N(0,1) init
  keeps initial p≈0.5±0.2; L_conf pushes AWAY from 0.5 so watch for
  premature bit-lock — if >95% bits are |p−0.5|>0.45 by epoch 100,
  re-init with smaller δ and re-run as v0.1 (record, don't hide).
